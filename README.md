**📈 Investing**

**💻 Productivity**

- **[Mac AI Checkup](https://github.com/Jianing1117/mac-ai-checkup)**<br>AI tools pile up, and so does what they leave behind. This gives your AI a checklist for cleaning up and tuning a Mac: biggest offenders first, Trash instead of rm, nothing deleted without asking.
- **[NotchPad](https://github.com/Jianing1117/notchpad)** · [Download](https://github.com/Jianing1117/notchpad/releases/latest)<br>A notepad that lives in the notch. To-dos, ideas, work in progress, laid out however you like. Hover to open, move away to close. Backed by Apple Reminders and Notes, so it syncs to your iPhone for free.

**🪞 Self-reflection**

- **[MindMirror](https://github.com/Jianing1117/jianing-skills/tree/main/mindmirror)**<br>Three ways of looking at yourself. *Mirror* reads what you've written and answers 22 hard questions about who you are now. *Digital Twin* interviews you and compiles a context file any AI can load. *Life Planning* maps the next five years across five dimensions, for anyone still in their odyssey years.

**✍️ Content creation**

## 🤝 Ground rules

- Only what I use myself, and still do.
- All code is public. No product pitches.
- Your data stays yours. The checkup runs in your browser; NotchPad stores everything in Apple's own Reminders and Notes.
- Nothing touches your files without asking. Deletes go to the Trash; MindMirror asks what it may read before it reads anything.
- As simple as the job allows, and no simpler.
