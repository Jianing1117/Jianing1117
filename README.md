### Productivity

- **[Mac AI Checkup](https://github.com/Jianing1117/mac-ai-checkup)**<br>AI tools pile up, and so does the junk they leave behind. This gives your AI the know-how to clean up your Mac without missing the big stuff or deleting the wrong thing.

## Ground rules

- I only share what I actually use and find worth it.
- All code is public. No product pitches.
- Your data stays yours. The financial checkup lives in your browser; NotchPad uses Apple's Reminders and Notes.
- Ask before touching your stuff. Mac AI Checkup only moves files to the Trash, and MindMirror asks what it may read first.
- Keep everything as simple as the job allows.
