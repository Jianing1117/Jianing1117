## Works

Grouped by what they're for, the same way as [WORKS](https://www.longarcsociety.com/works) on my site. The skills all live in the [jianing-skills](https://github.com/Jianing1117/jianing-skills) collection ![stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Jianing1117/jianing-skills?style=flat&label=stars&color=B8D9EA), and work with Claude Code, Codex, or any agent that reads `SKILL.md`.

### Finance

- **[Household Financial Checkup](https://github.com/Jianing1117/household-financial-checkup)** · [Try it online](https://www.longarcsociety.com/tools/checkup)<br>For a long time I thought investing meant trading stocks and doing VC deals. Only much later did I realize that asset allocation is one of the most important ideas in personal investing. This tool helps you see your household's financial health clearly and gives you a one-page family investment policy statement. No sign-up; your data stays in your own browser.

### Productivity

- **[Mac AI Checkup](https://github.com/Jianing1117/mac-ai-checkup)**<br>In the AI era, your computer fills up with the clutter AI leaves behind and with more and more tools. Give your own AI the know-how to clean up and tune your Mac, so it doesn't miss the biggest space hogs or delete the wrong things.
- **[NotchPad](https://github.com/Jianing1117/notchpad)** · [Download](https://github.com/Jianing1117/notchpad/releases/latest)<br>Lightweight sticky notes in your MacBook's notch. Set it up for your to-dos, everyday ideas, or the progress of your workflows. Hover over the notch and it opens; move away and it folds back. It syncs with Apple's own Reminders and Notes.

### Self-awareness

- **[MindMirror](https://github.com/Jianing1117/jianing-skills/tree/main/mindmirror)**<br>A full self-awareness kit for the AI era. "Mirror" looks at the present: it reads everything you've written and answers 22 sharp questions about who you are. "Digital Twin" looks at the past: through in-depth interviews and structured distillation, it builds your core context. "Life Planning" looks at the future: it maps out the next five years across five dimensions to help you find your way out of your odyssey years.

### Content creation

- **[Content Engine](https://github.com/Jianing1117/jianing-skills/tree/main/content-engine)**<br>Breaks down viral posts you admire and distills the patterns, helps you diagnose and edit your own drafts, then uses your post-publish data to refine the method.
- **[Content Positioning](https://github.com/Jianing1117/jianing-skills/tree/main/awesome-creator-positioning)**<br>Helps new creators on Douyin and Xiaohongshu find their direction: a one-line positioning, content boundaries, first content themes, and accounts to learn from. Run it once before you start.
- **[Whiteboard Cover](https://github.com/Jianing1117/jianing-skills/tree/main/awesome-creator-cover)**<br>Turns a title and key points into a whiteboard-style cover for Xiaohongshu or Douyin, as an editable Excalidraw file.
- **[IP Cartoon Guide](https://github.com/Jianing1117/jianing-skills/tree/main/ip-cartoon-guide)**<br>Turns one photo into a reusable cartoon character of you, then draws illustrations for your articles and projects with the same character.
- **[Super Slides](https://github.com/Jianing1117/jianing-skills/tree/main/super-slides)**<br>Settles the outline first, then the design, and gives you a web slide deck that's ready to present. Export to PDF or PPT when you need to.
- **[WeChat Formatter](https://github.com/Jianing1117/jianing-skills/tree/main/wechat-formatter)**<br>Picks a layout to suit the article and turns your Markdown into a version you can paste straight into the WeChat Official Account editor, plus a cover image.

## A few promises

- Only things I've used myself and found worthwhile go here.
- All the code is public, and nothing here recommends a product.
- Your data stays with you. The financial checkup only lives in your browser; NotchPad uses Apple's own Reminders and Notes.
- Ask before touching your stuff. Mac AI Checkup only moves things to the Trash, and MindMirror confirms what it may read before it reads anything.
- Every workflow and every line of code stays as simple as the job allows.

I write about the methods and stories behind these tools at [longarcsociety.com](https://www.longarcsociety.com/), in three tracks: harnessing AI, iterating on yourself, and compounding assets.
